Help & Documentation

Getting Started

To access the system, you will need an account. If you do not have one, click Sign Up on the login page and complete the registration form.


Once registered, log in with your username and password. You can check Remember Me to stay logged in between sessions.

Interview Page

The interview page displays a list of interview questions. For each question you can:


  • View the question and its scoring indicators
  • Enter a score between 1 and 6 for each indicator
  • Write notes in the Notes box
  • Write overall Feedback for the candidate's response

When you are finished, click Submit to save your responses. Your notes and feedback will be saved against each question.

Applications

The applications section allows you to view and manage candidate applications. Each application is linked to a pack and contains the candidate's answers to the pre-interview questions.


To submit an application on behalf of a candidate, select a pack from the applications page and complete the form.

Adding Questions

To add interview questions, navigate to Add Questions from the navigation bar. Select a category and fill in up to five questions at a time, then submit. Questions will appear on the interview page once added.


To manage existing questions, visit the Question List page where you can view and delete questions.

Notes

The home page allows you to create and view general notes. Notes are visible to all users and display the five most recent entries. To add a note, fill in the title and body fields and submit.

Your Account

You can manage your account from the Profile page. From here you can:


  • Change your password
  • View your account type
  • Delete your account
