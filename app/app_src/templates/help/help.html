Getting Started

To use the system you need an account. If you don't have one yet, click Sign Up on the login page and fill in your username, email address, and password.


Once you're logged in, what you can see depends on your role:


  • Candidates — can apply for roles and track their application status
  • Early Careers Definer (ECD) — can manage questions, packs, categories, indicators, and review results
  • Early Careers Assessment Manager (ECAM) — full access: everything ECD can do, plus running interviews and reviewing all applications

Tick Remember Me on the login page if you want to stay logged in between sessions.

Navigation

The navigation bar at the top of every page gives access to all sections. Some items only appear if your account has the right permissions.


  • Home — your dashboard (staff see stats and recent applications; candidates see their own application)
  • Applications — apply for a role (candidates) or review submitted applications (staff)
  • Inbox — accepted applicants ready to be interviewed
  • Results — interview scores and genre summary (staff only)
  • Candidate Dashboard — performance table across all candidates (staff only)
  • Questions > Add Question — add interview questions to the bank
  • Questions > View Questions — see all existing questions
  • Questions > Indicators — manage behavioural indicator pairs
  • Create Pack — set up a new application pack for a role
  • Create Category — add question categories
  • Your username (top right) — account settings
  • Dark/light toggle (top right) — switch between themes
  • Logout (top right) — sign out

Your Account

Click your username in the top-right corner to reach your account page. From here you can see your username, email, and role.


Changing your password:

  1. Enter your current password
  2. Enter your new password twice
  3. Click Update Password

Deleting your account: Click Delete Account and confirm with your password. This is permanent — all your data will be removed.

Home Page

What you see on the home page depends on your role.


Staff view shows:

  • Count of pending, accepted, and upcoming interviews
  • The five most recently submitted applications with their status
  • Quick links to the application review queue

Candidate view shows:

  • Your current application status (Pending, Accepted, or Denied)
  • Your interview date if one has been scheduled
  • A link to apply if you haven't submitted an application yet

Applying for a Role

The applications page lists all available packs. Each pack is linked to a role and contains up to three pre-interview questions for candidates to answer before their interview.


To submit a pre-interview form:

  1. Find the relevant pack on the applications page
  2. Click Open Pre-Interview Form
  3. Answer the questions provided
  4. Click Submit

You'll receive a status of Pending after submitting. Staff will review your application and either accept or deny it. If accepted, you'll see your interview date appear on your home page.

Reviewing Applications (Staff)

The application review page shows all pre-interview forms submitted by candidates, including their answers to each pre-interview question and the current status of their application.


To approve an application:

  1. Find the application in the list
  2. Click Approve — this moves the candidate to the Accepted Applicants (Inbox) page

To deny an application:

  1. Find the application in the list
  2. Click Deny — the status updates to Denied

Scheduling an Interview (Staff)

Once an application is approved, you can schedule an interview date from the Inbox page.


  1. Go to Inbox in the navigation bar
  2. Find the accepted candidate
  3. Click Schedule Interview
  4. Select the date and time using the date and time fields
  5. Click Schedule to confirm

The interview date will appear on the candidate's home page and on the interview card in the Inbox.

Running an Interview (Staff)

The interview page is where you score candidates during a live session. Each question has its own tab with a notes field, a score input, and a feedback field.


To run an interview:

  1. Open the interview for the candidate from the Inbox
  2. Work through each question tab — ask the question, then fill in Notes, Score, and Feedback
  3. If you want contextual guidance, click the ? button (bottom-left) to toggle one-liner hints next to each field
  4. Add behavioural indicators at the bottom once all questions are done (see Behavioural Indicators below)
  5. Click Submit when finished — all fields will be validated before submission

Score scale:

  • 1 — Very poor
  • 2 — Weak
  • 3 — Adequate
  • 4 — Good
  • 5 — Strong
  • 6 — Excellent

Autosave: The page automatically saves your progress every 30 seconds. You'll see a small "✓ Saved" indicator in the corner — you won't lose work if you need to pause mid-interview.


Score validation: If you try to submit with a score missing, the page will highlight the empty field and jump to the right tab automatically.

Behavioural Indicators

Behavioural indicators are traits you score holistically across the whole interview — not against individual questions. Each indicator has a name (e.g. Communication), a positive description (what the behaviour looks like when shown well), and a negative description (what it looks like when it's absent or poor).


Adding indicators to an interview:

  1. Scroll to the Interview Assessment section at the bottom of the interview page
  2. Select an indicator from the dropdown and click Add
  3. A scoring table appears — score each pair from 1–6 and add an overall group score
  4. Use the notes field to record what you observed

Managing indicators (staff only):

  1. Go to Questions > Indicators in the nav bar
  2. Enter an indicator name, add one or more positive/negative pairs, and click Save Indicator
  3. To delete a single pair, click the ✕ next to it
  4. To delete all pairs under a name, click Delete indicator next to the group heading and confirm

If you refresh the page with unsaved pairs in the form, a draft restore banner will appear — click Restore draft to reload them, or Discard to start fresh.

Results Page (Staff)

The results page gives a full view of all interview scores recorded in the system.


Genre summary table — at the top of the page, if questions have been tagged with a genre, this table shows the average score and total response count per behavioural genre. Use this to see which areas candidates are generally performing well or poorly in across all interviews.


Per-question breakdown — each question is listed as a card showing:

  • The average score across all candidates (shown as a coloured tag)
  • A table of every response: candidate name, score, notes, feedback, and when it was last updated

The Candidate Dashboard (also in the nav) shows a different view — a cross-candidate performance table you can filter by date range and sort by score.

Creating Application Packs (Staff)

A pack represents a role. It contains a description, a category (which links the pack to its interview questions), and up to three pre-interview questions for candidates to answer when applying.


To create a pack:

  1. Click Create Pack in the navigation bar
  2. Enter a title and description for the role
  3. Select the category — this determines which interview questions will be used when interviewing candidates who apply for this pack
  4. Add up to three pre-interview questions
  5. Click Save

The pack will immediately appear on the applications page for candidates to apply to.

Managing Interview Questions (Staff)

Interview questions are the questions asked during a live interview session. They are grouped by category and optionally tagged with a genre.


To add questions:

  1. Go to Questions > Add Question
  2. Enter up to five questions (only the first is required)
  3. Select a Category — questions will appear in any interview linked to a pack using this category
  4. Optionally select a Genre to tag the question with a behavioural area (e.g. Communication, Teamwork) — this feeds into the genre summary on the results page
  5. Click Save Questions

To delete a question, find it in the questions list and click Delete. This cannot be undone.

Categories & Genres (Staff)

Categories link packs to their interview questions. A pack is assigned one category, and all questions in that category will appear in the interview for any candidate who applies to that pack.


To create a new category, click Create Category in the nav bar, enter a name and optional description, and save.


Genres are a separate concept — they tag individual questions with a behavioural area (e.g. Communication, Safety Awareness, Teamwork). Unlike categories, genres don't affect which questions appear in an interview. They're used to group results on the results page so you can see how candidates perform across different behavioural areas.


Genres are managed through the admin panel or by contact with a system administrator. Once created, they appear as options when adding questions.
