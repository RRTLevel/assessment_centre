Getting Started

To use the system you will need an account. If you do not have one, click Sign Up on the login page. You will be asked to provide:


  • A username
  • An email address
  • An account type
  • A password

Once registered, return to the login page and enter your username and password. Tick Remember Me if you want to stay logged in between sessions.

Your Account

Your account page shows your username, email address, and account type.


To change your password, enter your current password followed by your new password twice, then click Update Password.


To delete your account, click Delete Account and confirm by entering your password in the prompt. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Interview Page

The interview page shows all active interview questions. For each question, enter scores, notes, and feedback, then click Submit to save.

Applications

The applications page shows all available packs. Each pack represents a role and contains a set of pre-interview questions. Click Open Pre-Interview Form on a pack to answer its questions and submit.

Adding Questions

Navigate to Add Question in the navigation bar. You can add up to five questions at a time and assign them to a category. Click Save Questions to submit. Added questions will appear on the interview page.
