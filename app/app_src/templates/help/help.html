Getting Started

To use the system you will need an account. If you do not have one, click Sign Up on the login page. You will be asked to provide:


  • Username — your unique login name
  • Email address — used for account identification
  • Account type — your role (e.g. Assessor, Early Careers Definer)
  • Password — must be at least 8 characters

Once registered, return to the login page and enter your username and password. Tick Remember Me if you want to stay logged in between sessions.

Navigation

The navigation bar at the top of every page gives you access to all sections of the system:


  • Home — your notes board
  • Interview Questions — score and annotate interview questions
  • Applications — view and submit pre-interview forms
  • Create Pack — create a new application pack for a role
  • Add Question — add new interview questions
  • Create Category — add question categories
  • Application Review — review submitted applications
  • Your username (top right) — view and manage your account
  • Logout (top right) — sign out of the system

Your Account

Click your username in the top-right of the navigation bar to access your account page. Here you can see your username, email address, and account type.


Changing your password:

  1. Enter your current password
  2. Enter your new password twice
  3. Click Update Password

Logging out:

Click Logout in the top-right of the navigation bar to sign out. If you did not tick Remember Me when logging in, you will be logged out automatically when you close the browser.


Deleting your account:

Click Delete Account and confirm by entering your password. This is permanent and cannot be undone — all your data will be removed.

Home Page

The home page is your personal notes board. You can write short notes visible to all users — useful for leaving reminders or observations during an assessment day.


Adding a note:

  1. Enter a title for your note
  2. Enter the note body
  3. Click Add Note to save

The five most recent notes are shown on the page.

Interview Page

The interview page shows all active interview questions. Each question has its own scoring table, notes field, and feedback field.


Scoring a question:

  1. Read the question shown at the top of each card
  2. For each indicator row in the table, enter a score between 1 and 6 in the Score column
  3. Use the Notes field to record anything observed during the interview for that question
  4. Use the Feedback field to write your overall assessment for that question

Scoring scale:

  • 1–2 — below expectation
  • 3–4 — meets expectation
  • 5–6 — exceeds expectation

Once you have filled in all questions, scroll to the bottom and click Submit. Your responses are saved and can be updated at any time by returning to this page and submitting again.

Results

The results page shows a summary of all submitted interview responses across all assessors.


Each question is displayed as a card containing a table with:

  • User — the assessor who submitted the response
  • Score 1, 2, 3 — scores entered for each indicator row
  • Notes — notes recorded during the interview
  • Feedback — overall feedback for the question
  • Last Updated — when the response was last saved

Questions with no responses yet will show a message indicating no data has been submitted.

Applications

The applications page lists all available application packs. Each pack represents a role and contains up to three pre-interview questions for candidates to answer before their interview.


Submitting a pre-interview form:

  1. Find the relevant pack on the applications page
  2. Click Open Pre-Interview Form
  3. Answer the questions provided
  4. Click Submit to send your answers

Submitted applications can be reviewed and approved or denied by your assessor.

Application Review

The application review page shows all pre-interview forms submitted by candidates. Each entry shows the applicant's username, the pack they applied for, their answers, and the current status of their application.


Approving an application:

  1. Find the application in the list
  2. Click Approve
  3. Select an interview date from the calendar
  4. Click Confirm to save — the application status will update to Accepted

Denying an application:

  1. Find the application in the list
  2. Click Deny
  3. The application status will update to Denied

Adding Questions

Navigate to Add Question in the navigation bar to add new interview questions to the system.


Adding questions:

  1. Enter up to five questions in the fields provided (only the first is required)
  2. Select a category to group the questions under
  3. Click Save Questions to submit

Added questions will immediately appear on the interview page for all users.
